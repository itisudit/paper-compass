About the practice

Broad reading is not deep reading.

Most literature workflows optimize for finding, summarizing, and storing. Paper Compass is designed for the consequential paper: the one you need to interrogate before you let it change your thinking.

Find → orient → select → interrogate → judge → remember

Two speeds of research

Broad reading

Fast · wide · selective

Use discovery, screening, AI-assisted synthesis, and source organization to understand the landscape and decide which papers deserve more of your attention.

Google Scholar · Elicit · Zotero · Gemini Notebook

Deep reading

Slow · narrow · critical

For high-impact papers, establish context, interrogate methods and evidence, reach your own conclusion, and only then compare it with the authors’ interpretation.

Paper Compass · seven-step critical reading

The seven-step method

Context → Question → Gap → Methods & power → Independent verdict → Reconciliation → Limits & confounders

Core principle: No AI interpretation before your independent verdict.

Why the distinction matters

Broad reading helps you move through a large literature quickly. Deep reading is reserved for the papers that can materially change your research question, interpretation, or methods.

Phase 1 · Aerial view

Gather context before you get lost in details.

01

Get a broad overview

Scan the abstract, figures and legends. What kind of study is this, and what does its overall design try to establish?

02

Name the core question

Find the defining question at the end of the introduction. State the hypothesis precisely enough to check every later claim against it.

03

Map the knowledge gap

What is already known? What missing piece does this work address? Why would closing that gap matter?


Phase 2 · Interrogation

Evaluate whether the evidence can answer the question.

04

Appraise methods & power

Are the models, sampling, controls and analysis appropriate? Look for a power analysis, accessible raw data, and reproducible code or pipelines.

05

Reach your conclusion first

Skip the discussion. Read results, graphs, tables and error bars. What do the data actually support—before you read the authors’ interpretation?

    Find confounders & limits
