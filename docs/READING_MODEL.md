# Reading Model

## Cognitive loop

**Think → Inspect → Question → Connect → Challenge → Revise → Judge**

The reader forms an initial understanding, checks it against the paper, identifies questions, connects ideas, challenges claims or assumptions, revises their thinking, and reaches a judgement.

## Triage

Triage is about allocating attention, not judging the quality of the paper.

The reader decides whether to:

* **Surf** — get the intellectual shape of the paper
* **Swim** — understand and critically evaluate it
* **Dive Deep** — thoroughly interrogate it
* **Park for later** — defer attention until the paper or timing makes it worthwhile

The reader can move between depths as their interest or needs change.

Parking is a deferral decision, not a depth level.

## Reading journey

### 1. Orient

What is this paper trying to establish?

### 2. Place

What conversation is it entering?

### 3. Reconstruct

How does it try to answer its question?

### 4. Appraise

Is the approach appropriate?

### 5. Test

What does the evidence actually establish?

### 6. Connect

How does it fit with wider knowledge?

### 7. Judge

What do I conclude?

These stages are guides. They should not become a rigid checklist or require seven separate screens.

## When the reader is stuck

Being stuck is part of serious reading.

Paper Compass should help the reader identify whether they:

- don't understand the paper
- don't understand a concept or method
- don't know what to look for
- disagree with something or think something may be wrong

Help should come progressively:

**Nudge → Direction → Explanation → Deeper assistance**

The system should support the reader's reasoning rather than replace it.
