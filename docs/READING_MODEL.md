# Reading Model

## Cognitive loop

**Think → Inspect → Question → Connect → Challenge → Revise → Judge**

The reader forms an initial understanding, checks it against the paper, identifies questions, connects ideas, challenges claims or assumptions, revises their thinking, and reaches a judgement.

## Triage

Triage is about allocating attention, not judging the quality of the paper.

The reader decides whether to:

- Surf — get the intellectual shape of the paper
- Swim — understand the reasoning and evidence
- Dive Deep — interrogate the paper and form a considered judgement
- Shore — defer attention until the paper or timing makes it worthwhile

These are attention depths, not difficulty levels. The reader can move between them as their interest or needs change.

Shore is a deferral decision, not a depth level.

## Reading sequences

The same intellectual journey is approached at different depths.

+ ## Depth philosophy
+
+ Surf, Swim, and Dive Deep are different ways of engaging with the same intellectual journey, not levels defined by the number of questions or tasks.
+
+ Surf supports selective attention and orientation.
+
+ Swim supports active understanding through questioning, prediction, inspection, comparison, and revision.
+
+ Dive Deep supports sustained scientific interrogation, reconstruction, critical appraisal, connection, and independent judgement.
+
+ The interaction should reduce the cognitive effort required to begin and sustain serious thinking without reducing the intellectual demands of the reading.

## Reading sequences

The same intellectual journey is approached at different depths.

### Surf

Orient → Place → Judge

The goal is to get the paper's basic shape and decide whether it is worth deeper attention.

### Swim

Orient → Place → Reconstruct → Appraise → Test → Judge

The goal is to understand the paper's reasoning and evidence and form a considered conclusion.

### Dive Deep

Orient → Place → Reconstruct → Appraise → Test → Connect → Judge

The goal is to thoroughly interrogate the paper, connect it to wider knowledge, and form an independent judgement.

The routes are guides rather than rigid checklists. Depth determines which stages the reader encounters and how much attention and scaffolding each stage receives.

## Reading environment

After triage, the reader enters a focused reading environment.

The paper and the reader's thinking remain present together. The reader should be able to inspect the paper, form their own understanding, and record their thinking without losing the context of the reading session.

Paper Compass presents one meaningful intellectual question at a time rather than asking the reader to work through a large checklist.

The reader's responses become part of the developing interpretation of the paper. Earlier thinking should remain available as the reader moves forward and revises their understanding.

The system should guide the reading without determining what the reader should conclude.

## Reading journey

### 1. Orient

What is this paper trying to establish?

### 2. Place

What conversation is it entering?

### 3. Reconstruct

How does it try to answer its question?

### 4. Appraise

Is the approach appropriate?

### 5. Test

What does the evidence actually establish?

### 6. Connect

How does it fit with wider knowledge?

### 7. Judge

What do I conclude?

These stages are guides. They should not become a rigid checklist or require seven separate screens.

## When the reader is stuck

Being stuck is part of serious reading.

Paper Compass should help the reader identify whether they:

- don't understand the paper
- don't understand a concept or method
- don't know what to look for
- disagree with something or think something may be wrong

Help should come progressively:

**Nudge → Direction → Explanation → Deeper assistance**

The system should support the reader's reasoning rather than replace it.
