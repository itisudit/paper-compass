# Design Principles

- Reduce cognitive load without reducing intellectual load.
- One meaningful question or action at a time.
- Keep the paper central.
- Think before being told, where practical.
- Guide, don't drive.
- Scaffold, don't solve.
- Reduce friction, not rigour.
- Curiosity over gamification.
- Progress means understanding, not time spent.
- Uncertainty and confusion can be useful.
- Allow wandering, pausing, and returning.
- The product should become quiet when the reader is deeply engaged.
